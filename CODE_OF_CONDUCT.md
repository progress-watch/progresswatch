# Code of conduct

Be decent to each other. Concretely:

- Assume the person on the other side is trying to help, including when they are wrong.
- Criticise the code, the design or the decision — not the person who wrote it.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or comments about who someone is rather than what they
  said. No sexualised language or imagery. No posting anyone's private information.
- If someone asks a question that seems obvious, it was not obvious to them. Answer it or
  say nothing.

This applies to issues, discussions, pull requests and commit messages — everywhere the
project is.

If someone is behaving this way, report it through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/progress-watch/progresswatch/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainers directly, and it will be handled privately. Maintainers
may edit, hide or delete comments, and block accounts, when it is warranted.
